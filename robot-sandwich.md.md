# M1LAB2: The Robot Sandwich Instructions

## Assumptions
- The robot is standing in front of a clean kitchen counter.
- The robot's hands are empty and clean.
- A loaf of sliced bread in a plasticbag with a twist-tie is on counter.
- Peanut butter jar with screw lid is on counter.
- Jelly jar with screw lid is on counter.
- Butter knife and plate are on counter.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
- Twist the bread bag twist-tie counter-clockwise to remove it.
- Open the plastic bread bag with your hands.
- Take two slices of bread out of the bag.
- Place both bread slices flat, side-by-side, on the plate.
- Unscrew the peanut butter jar lid counter-clockwise and set it down.
- Pick up the butter knife by the handle.
- Scoop one tablespoon of peanut butter out with the knife.
- Spread the peanut butter evenly onto the first slice of bread.
- Put the knife down and screw the peanut butter lid back on clockwise.
- Unscrew the jelly jar lid counter-clockwise and set it down.
- Pick up the butter knife by the handle.
- Scoop one tablespoon of jelly out with the knife.
- Spread the jelly evenly onto the first slice of bread.
- Put the knife down and screw the jelly lid back on clockwise.
- Pick up the peanut butter slice by its crust.
- Flip it over and place it directly on top of the jelly slice.

## Done When
The robot hands are empty and two slices of bread are stacked together on the plate with peanut butter and jelly on the inside.
